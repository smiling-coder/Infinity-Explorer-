PLANETARY EXPLORATION

Meet Our Eight Planets

Travel from the Sun's closest planet to the distant ice giant Neptune. Select a planet's image source to explore the original NASA image collection.

01 / INNER PLANET

Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet and the closest planet to the Sun. Its heavily cratered surface resembles our Moon. Mercury has almost no atmosphere to retain heat, so temperatures change dramatically between day and night.

Type: Terrestrial planet

Diameter: About 4,879 km

Year: 88 Earth days

Moons: 0

Explore Mercury at NASA ↗
02 / INNER PLANET

Venus

Venus is Earth's nearest planetary neighbour in size. Its thick atmosphere is rich in carbon dioxide, and its clouds contain sulfuric acid. A powerful greenhouse effect makes Venus the hottest planet in our solar system.

Type: Terrestrial planet

Diameter: About 12,104 km

Year: 225 Earth days

Moons: 0

Explore Venus at NASA ↗
03 / OUR HOME

Earth

Earth is our home and the only planet currently known to support life. Its liquid-water oceans, protective atmosphere, magnetic field, and suitable temperatures make it an extraordinary world for living organisms.

Type: Terrestrial planet

Diameter: About 12,742 km

Year: 365.25 days

Moons: 1

Explore Earth at NASA ↗
04 / THE RED PLANET

Mars

Mars is a cold desert world famous for its reddish surface, coloured by iron-rich minerals. It has giant volcanoes, deep canyons, polar ice caps, and evidence that liquid water existed on its surface in the distant past.

Type: Terrestrial planet

Diameter: About 6,779 km

Year: 687 Earth days

Moons: 2

Explore Mars at NASA ↗
05 / GAS GIANT

Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system. It is a gas giant composed mainly of hydrogen and helium. Its famous Great Red Spot is a gigantic storm, and its many moons include Europa, a world of great scientific interest.

Type: Gas giant

Diameter: About 139,820 km

Year: About 11.86 Earth years

Moons: Many confirmed moons

Explore Jupiter at NASA ↗
06 / RINGED GIANT

Saturn

Saturn is famous for its spectacular ring system, made mainly of countless pieces of water ice, rocky material, and dust. It is a gas giant with a low average density and fascinating moons, including Titan and Enceladus.

Type: Gas giant

Diameter: About 116,460 km

Year: About 29.4 Earth years

Moons: Many confirmed moons

Explore Saturn at NASA ↗
07 / ICE GIANT

Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant with a blue-green colour caused by methane in its atmosphere. Its rotation axis is tilted dramatically, making it appear to spin almost on its side. It has faint rings and an extremely cold atmosphere.

Type: Ice giant

Diameter: About 50,724 km

Year: About 84 Earth years

Moons: Many confirmed moons

Explore Uranus at NASA ↗
08 / OUTER PLANET

Neptune

Neptune is the eighth and most distant planet from the Sun. This ice giant has a deep blue appearance and extremely powerful winds. Its distant orbit takes almost 165 Earth years to complete one revolution around the Sun.

Type: Ice giant

Diameter: About 49,244 km

Year: About 165 Earth years

Moons: 16 known moons

Explore Neptune at NASA ↗
